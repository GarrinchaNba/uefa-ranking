## Revised design decisions and evidence gate (2026-11-07)

**Decision — windows and retention**: For selected season S, calculate rankings for S-4..S and independently for S-5..S-1. Retain details for each selectable season, plus older season coefficients, season-specific membership and tie-break inputs needed for both windows. The initial proposed selectable fixtures are 2023/24 and 2026/27; the earliest preceding comparison requires inputs for 2018/19. Older coefficient-only seasons are not automatically selectable.

**Rationale**: Prior rank is not recoverable from the five displayed columns alone. Retaining full events for every historical input year is unnecessary if that year is not selectable. **Alternative rejected**: assuming a new entrant's previous rank or calculating movement from sorted row indices would fabricate movement.

**Decision — scoring paths**: Compute association-eligible awards independently of club awards and divide by distinct participating clubs under each season's verified rules. Group association-detail subtotals by the club-point entry's competition; label them as club subtotals, not association coefficients.

**Rationale**: Club minimum guarantees, qualifying points and eligibility may differ. **Alternative rejected**: dividing displayed club coefficients or competition subtotals by a participant count conflates two scoring systems and duplicates transferred clubs.

**Directly checked 2026/27 association rules**: UEFA's [association coefficient overview](https://www.uefa.com/nationalassociations/uefarankings/country/about/) reproduces Annex D.3, D.5, D.7 and D.8. Ordinarily the season divisor is the number of participating clubs, counted once across competitions. However, D.3 requires the number of clubs the association was entitled to enter under the access list if a qualified club refused entry, was excluded or not admitted, and was not replaced by another club from that association. Match points are normally 2/1/0, halved in qualifying and play-offs; D.7 specifies different points for certain rounds changed from two legs to one, excluding the finals. Bonus eligibility and the club guarantees require their own rule handling. Association ties use the most recent unequal season coefficient first. This verifies the current-season overview, not the unexamined historical rules.

**Worked current-season divisor check**: For an association whose eligible points total 10 and whose applicable divisor is 3, its season coefficient is `3.333`, truncated to thousandths. If an applicable D.3 non-replacement exception changes the divisor to 4, the coefficient is `2.5`, not `3.333`. Neither calculation uses club-specific guaranteed minima.

## Scoring and ranking

**Decision**: Model sporting club-coefficient rules by season and competition, including game eligibility, bonus awards, guaranteed minimums as non-duplicated adjustments, tie-breaks, and coefficient precision. Both rankings use the selected season and four preceding seasons; the active-season view is labelled provisional.

**Rationale**: [UEFA's club coefficient rules](https://www.uefa.com/nationalassociations/uefarankings/club/about/) specify season-specific regulations, the higher of a club's five-season sum and 20% of its association's five-season coefficient, thousandth precision without rounding up, and ordered tie-break criteria. UEFA's fixed 2026/27 seeding ranking uses 2021/22–2025/26, whereas the requested rolling view uses 2022/23–2026/27. Official seeding and the requested provisional view therefore differ.

**Alternatives considered**: A single universal win/draw formula would misapply historical rules; presenting the provisional view as UEFA's official seeding ranking would mislead users.

**Implementation gate**: Verify the candidate season-specific schedules below against primary sources before implementation; do not carry the league-phase format or awards backward into group-stage seasons. Historical mock contributions may be synthetic but must be clearly labelled and consistent with their verified declared rules. Do not invent unavailable historical results or mark incomplete totals verified.

Worked checks directly from the cited 2026/27 rules: a Champions League league-phase win yields 2 match points; a Europa League club with 1 win, 1 draw, and 6 defeats has 3 league-phase points, so the 3-point guarantee adds no extra points; a Conference League club with 1 win and 6 defeats has 2 earned points and a separately attributable 0.5 minimum adjustment to reach 2.5. A coefficient floor example with an association coefficient of 14.999 gives `floor(14.999 × 0.2 × 1000) / 1000 = 2.999`, not 3.000.

#### Match and elimination points

- **2022/23–2026/27**: From the group/league phase onward, a ratified win normally earns 2 points, a draw 1, and a defeat 0; applicable knockout play-offs are excluded from club match points. The 2023/24 copy's rules expressly assigned to 2022/23 exclude Europa/Conference knockout-round play-offs; the 2024/25 copy excludes knockout-phase play-offs for all competitions. Penalty-shootout outcomes are not used; the match result before the shoot-out applies. Special single-leg rules may alter points.
- **2022/23 and 2023/24, legacy format**: Qualifying results do not earn club match points for clubs that advance. A club eliminated from the Champions League or Europa League qualifying rounds is credited under the lower competition it enters. Conference League eliminations earn 1 point in qualifying round 1, 1.5 in round 2, 2 in round 3, and 2.5 in the play-offs.
- **2024/25–2026/27, league-phase format**: The same Conference League qualifying-elimination schedule applies. Champions League/Europa League qualifying eliminations are credited under the lower competition entered, rather than as an additional award in the competition of elimination.

#### Stage/position bonuses and minima

- **2022/23 and 2023/24**: Champions League group-stage participation earns 4 bonus points. Europa League clubs have a 3-point group-stage minimum; Conference League clubs have a 2.5-point minimum. Those are floors, not additive participation points: add only `max(0, minimum - eligible match points)` as a separately identified adjustment. Europa League group winners/runners-up earn 4/2 points; Conference League group winners/runners-up earn 2/1. Progression bonuses for reaching the round of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals, and final are respectively 5/1/1/1 in the Champions League, 1/1/1/1 in the Europa League, and 0/0/1/1 in the Conference League.
- **2024/25–2026/27**: There is no group-stage participation/placement award. UEFA's D.5 league-phase final-position bonus table is competition-specific: Champions League ranks 1–24 receive `12.000 - 0.250 × (rank - 1)` and ranks 25–36 receive `6.000`; Europa League ranks 1–24 receive `6.000 - 0.250 × (rank - 1)` and ranks 25–36 receive `0`; Conference League ranks 1–9 receive `4.000 - 0.250 × (rank - 1)`, ranks 10–24 receive `2.000 - 0.125 × (rank - 9)`, and ranks 25–36 receive `0`. These reproduce every row in the official table, including rank-1 values of 12/6/4 and the 6-point Champions League award for ranks 25–36. Europa and Conference League clubs also have league-phase minima of 3 and 2.5 points, with the same non-additive floor treatment. Knockout-round participation bonuses are 1.5/1/1/1 for Champions League round of 16/quarter-final/semi-final/final, 1/1/1/1 for Europa League, and 0.5/0.5/0.5/0.5 for Conference League. Knockout-phase play-off match results themselves do not add win/draw points.

#### Coefficients, guarantees, and ties

- **Association coefficient**: Add association points (including association-eligible bonuses) across that association's clubs and divide by the number of participating clubs for that season; qualifying match points for association coefficients use the applicable half-point rules. Do not derive it from a club-ranking country aggregate. The league-phase club guaranteed minimum is excluded from the association coefficient.
- **Five-season club coefficient**: Sum the club's five season coefficients and compare with 20% of its association's five-season association coefficient; use the higher value. Coefficients are truncated, not rounded, to three decimal places. For an association coefficient of `14.999`, the floor is `truncate(14.999 × 0.20, 3) = 2.999`, not `3.000`.
- **Tie-breaks**: For equal club coefficients, compare each club's coefficients in the most recent season first, then the next most recent season in which they differ; if still equal, compare the association coefficient, then domestic championship position in the most recent season. These are the UEFA D.8 order in the 2026/27 Annex D linked above.

#### Worked rule checks

1. In either legacy season, a Champions League group-stage win contributes 2 match points plus the separate 4-point participation bonus, before later-round bonuses.
2. In either legacy season, an Europa League group record of 1 win, 1 draw, and 4 defeats has 3 match points; the 3-point minimum adds nothing. If the club finishes runner-up, its 2-point placement bonus is separate, for 5 total points before later-round bonuses.
3. In either legacy season, a Conference League group record with 1 win and 5 defeats earns 2 match points, so the 2.5 minimum contributes a 0.5 adjustment. A group-winner bonus, if applicable, is a distinct 2-point entry.
4. In a league-phase season, an Europa League club with 1 win, 1 draw, and 6 defeats has 3 match points and no additional guarantee before its position bonus. A Conference League club with 1 win and 6 defeats has 2 match points plus a 0.5 guarantee adjustment before its position bonus.
5. In the 2026/27 D.5 table, rank 1 earns league-position bonuses of 12/6/4 points in the Champions/Europa/Conference League, Europa rank 24 earns 0.250, and Conference rank 10 earns 1.875. In all supported seasons, an association floor of `14.999` yields `2.999` after the 20% operation and thousandth truncation. For a tie, compare the latest-season contribution before moving back through the ordered five-season period.

## Association totals versus club totals

**Decision**: Maintain association-coefficient inputs independently of the competition-group club subtotals. Apply the 20% floor to the five-season club sum only; show a separate minimum adjustment if it applies. For this project's rolling view, the association input uses the same selected-plus-four-season window as the club sum; the active-season view is not UEFA's fixed current-season seeding coefficient.

**Rationale**: [UEFA's association coefficient rules](https://www.uefa.com/nationalassociations/uefarankings/country/about/) use association-specific points and a participating-club divisor. Qualifying results and club-specific guaranteed minimums can be treated differently from club coefficients, so the country row is not an association coefficient.

**Alternative considered**: Deriving the association coefficient from the displayed country total would conflate two different scoring systems.

## Precision, ordering, and missing values

**Decision**: Use exact decimal or scaled-integer calculations with explicit UEFA truncation at the rule-defined boundaries, and serialize coefficients as decimal strings. Order complete five-season records by coefficient and UEFA's applicable ordered tie-breaks. Display unresolved ties as tied or unranked, and incomplete records separately.

**Rationale**: Binary floating-point and fabricated zeros can change rankings or hide unknown inputs. A known non-participating season is zero; an unavailable season or association input is not.

**Alternative considered**: Floating-point numbers and a default zero simplify code but do not preserve the required ranking semantics.

## Competition stage display

**Decision**: Give each competition/season a stage schedule and map each game to an ordered semantic column key and slot. Provide shared column definitions for comparable stages; omit nonexistent games and explicitly distinguish pending games from confirmed zero-point results.

**Rationale**: The competitions differ in matchday counts and knockout formats. A common positional array alone cannot safely align rounds or distinguish a blank from a loss.

**Alternative considered**: One fixed match index per club would misalign comparable knockout stages.

## Providers, storage, and status

**Decision**: Use a typed mock provider with an injected, explicit active-season value and fixed synthetic fixtures. A future live provider will have its own validation, provenance, clock, and cache policy; MongoDB will be introduced only with that integration. Return explicit `mock`, `live`, `stale`, `partial`, or `unavailable` status rather than a success-shaped fallback.

**Rationale**: The initial release is deterministic, offline, and credential-free, while a production cache needs agreed provider terms and update/freshness policies. MongoDB's [TTL indexes](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/manual/core/index-ttl/) are cleanup mechanisms, not proof that cached data is fresh.

**Alternatives considered**: Requiring MongoDB for fixed mock data adds operational burden without cache value; inventing live-source cadence or source availability would overstate what is known.

## Frontend accessibility and tests

**Decision**: Use semantic tables and the [WAI-ARIA tabs pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/tabs/), with tab-to-panel relationships, keyboard support, visible focus, and text labels for bonus/adjustment/status. Test pure rules, HTTP contracts, UI interactions, and one full browser flow.

**Rationale**: The specification requires keyboard access and explicit distinctions that must remain understandable without color.

**Alternative considered**: Color-only indicators and mouse-only tab controls would violate the constitution and make the UI less usable.

## Tooling

**Decision**: Use Node.js 22 LTS, npm workspaces, React/Vite/TypeScript/Tailwind, Express, Vitest, React Testing Library, Supertest, and Playwright; lock mutually compatible versions during implementation.

**Rationale**: These match the requested stack and cover unit, integration, and end-to-end validation without introducing unrelated infrastructure. The current environment reports Node 22.22.1 and npm 9.2.0; runtime compatibility must be verified against selected package versions.

**Alternative considered**: Separate uncoordinated dependency trees would make shared API contracts and repeatable commands harder to maintain.
