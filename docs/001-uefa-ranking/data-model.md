# Data Model: UEFA Club and Association Rankings

This is the target domain model, not the current snapshot implementation. Use stable IDs, season-specific facts and source attribution. Do not equate missing records with a zero.

**Migration status**: `RankingSnapshotSchema` and `getValidatedRankingSnapshot` validate season-specific membership, round/game, attributed award and divisor fields on the synthetic provider snapshot. The global club and association ranking routes now calculate rolling five-season views; the association scoring engine checks supported match and qualifying points, divisor evidence, and exact thousandth truncation. Association competition details group club awards without converting them into association coefficients. The UI shares season selection between keyboard-operable club/country tabs. Historical coefficient inputs and the fixture remain synthetic, incomplete and not UEFA-verified; active associations with incomplete award coverage or divisors remain partial/unranked. Club ranking retains its explicitly tagged fixture-only assumptions and associated `FIXME(mvp-...)` disclosures; they do not apply to snapshots with non-fixture source IDs.

The synthetic active-season fixture includes qualifying eliminations followed by lower-competition participation. Qualifying results have separate association awards rather than club match points. In each selectable season a club eliminated in the Conference League first qualifying round has two ratified qualifying games and a reconciled one-point club elimination award, not association bonus points. Its association has a complete, independently calculated zero-point season coefficient with a one-club divisor. The historical fixture has complete association inputs that demonstrate a rank reversal and complete club inputs on both sides of the association floor; this is synthetic arithmetic, not a verified ranking endpoint. Most fixture records remain partial rather than representing complete competition histories.

Each stored season declares rule-support evidence separately for club scoring, association scoring and ranking; `unsupported` means calculations must not claim verification. The `verified` marker records research evidence, not the existence of an implemented scoring engine or verified results. The 2021/22 and 2023/24 club and association scoring markers cite user-supplied regulation copies; historical ranking support and seasons without verified category-wide evidence remain unsupported.

Coefficient comparison is a separate state from rule support and rank resolution: a same-entity, same-measure, same-period and same-snapshot UEFA reference may be `matched`, `different`, or `unavailable`/incomparable. Only a demonstrated difference receives the italic ambiguous-coefficient indicator; exact agreement removes that indicator for that value without proving an ambiguous rule or rank. Unavailable references remain explicitly unverified.

## Computation and State

1. Validate identifiers, cross-references, duplicate identities, selectable seasons and data availability at the provider boundary. Represent unknown inputs explicitly. Do not require nonexistent clubs to have fabricated complete records in every stored historical season.
2. Select the current and prior five-season windows for the requested selected season. Calculate the two global populations independently, using that season's membership and applicable verified tie-breaks. A missing prior rank or unresolved tie produces unavailable movement.
3. Derive club season points from eligible ratified events and separately attributed bonuses/adjustments when details exist. A selected season's expanded detail is available, partial, missing, or confirmed absence; a final detail total is never claimed complete if its breakdown cannot reconcile.
4. Compute association-eligible event totals and the applicable season-specific divisor, normally distinct participating clubs but subject to verified exceptions, applying precision at documented boundaries. An attributed historical coefficient input may support older windows without game details but remains explicitly synthetic or sourced.
5. Calculate complete rolling coefficients and ranks. For current incomplete-but-observed data, any provisional presentation must be labelled and must not imply final/verified rankings. Never give a definitive verified rank when missing values or unresolved ties can change it, including missing values belonging to other competitors.
6. Build competition group subtotals from club awards uniquely attributed to that competition, separately from association-eligible entries. Sort UI rows by season contribution independently of the global rank and movement.

## Retention and Fixture Requirements

- Every selectable season retains its game/round/bonus/adjustment details or explicit per-entity unavailability; pre-window coefficient-only seasons are not automatically selectable.
- Initial synthetic selectable seasons `2023-24` and `2026-27` require stored inputs from at least `2018-19` through `2026-27`; the 2023/24 previous comparison uses `2018-19`–`2022-23`.
- Fixtures need sufficient complete examples for both rankings and both club-floor branches, plus transfers, missing/unknown cases, non-participation, ties and all movement states. Synthetic data is not actual UEFA history.
- Provider data and responses use decimal strings and exact arithmetic; values, event IDs, membership, provenance and precision are validated against applicable rules.
